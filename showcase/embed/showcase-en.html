What we specialise in

Five specialisms. A dedicated team for each.

EchoLight is built around five disciplines, and each one has its own specialist team. Try them below: every lab ends with a message to us describing what you built.

Stage Lighting

Moving heads, washes and pattern projection, programmed cue by cue so the room changes with the moment. From conference stages to ballroom wedding floors.

  • Moving heads
  • Gobo patterns
  • Architectural light
  • Cue programming

LED Screens

Sound
